Privacy

Outline — last updated November 20, 2026. This page will be completed as the app ships.

Couchquizzer is a party trivia game for Apple TV. The plan is for people in the same room to join from their phones over the local Wi-Fi network. There is no account to create, and the game is not designed to need a subscription or a central game server.

What stays on your devices

Room codes, answers, and scores are meant to stay on the Apple TV and the phones in the room — traffic on your local network, not a service we run in the cloud. We do not ask you to sign in.

Local network

To let phones find and join a game, the Apple TV may advertise a room on the local network (for example, using Bonjour). That is only so nearby devices on the same Wi-Fi can connect to the host in the room. It is not used to build a location history or an account profile.

This website

This site is a static page. We do not set cookies or add analytics here, and the site does not collect personal information about you.

Children

We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The game is meant to be played together, in person, with people you already know.

Changes

If the app’s behavior changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@couchquizzer.com. That reaches Edward Dunn. You can also use the support page.
