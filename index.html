How it works

  1. Launch on Apple TV

    Start a game in the living room. The TV hosts the room and shows a 4-letter code on the big screen.

  2. Join on your phone

    Everyone on the same Wi-Fi enters the room code and plays from their phone. No account to create.

  3. Race the clock for the crown

    Answer before time runs out. Faster correct answers score more, and the TV keeps the live scoreboard.

Built for game night

Local Wi-Fi play

The Apple TV hosts the game on your home network. There is no subscription and no game server in the middle.

Curated categories

Play from a bundled bank of timeless questions across science, geography, history, arts, sports, and movies & music.

Apple TV + iPhone

Scores, the room code, and the podium stay on the TV. Everyone else just needs their phone on the same Wi-Fi.
