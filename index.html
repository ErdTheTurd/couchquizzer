The scores stay on the TV. Your phone is the buzzer.

Start a game on Apple TV. A 4-letter code goes up on the screen. Everyone in the room joins over the same Wi-Fi. Each question lasts 12 seconds, and leftover time is extra points.

Coming soon to Apple TV

A 4-letter code on the TV

The Apple TV hosts the room and puts a code like QUIZ on the screen. Phones on the same Wi-Fi join with that code and act as buzzers.

A round on the big screen

The TV asks the question, shows who got it, keeps the running score, and finishes on a podium.

How you play

One Apple TV in the room. Phones as buzzers. Nothing to set up in the cloud.

  1. 1

    Launch on Apple TV

    The TV hosts the room and puts a 4-letter code on the screen, like QUIZ.

  2. 2

    Join on your phone

    Same Wi-Fi, type the code. Your phone is the buzzer. No account to create.

  3. 3

    Race the 12-second clock

    A correct answer is 100 points, plus 10 for every second left. The TV keeps score and ends on a podium.

What’s in the game

  • Local Wi-Fi only

    The Apple TV hosts the room on your home network. There is no subscription and no game server in the middle.

  • Curated questions

    A bundled bank of timeless questions in science, geography, history, arts & literature, sports, and movies & music.

  • Built for the living room

    Made for Apple TV, with phones on the same Wi-Fi. You can also run a solo or demo game from the TV itself.
